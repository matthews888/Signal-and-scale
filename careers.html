UGC Creator

Full-time, ongoingFully remoteWorldwide applications$200–$300 per 60-second video

Join our team as a full-time UGC Creator and make natural, engaging videos for brands from wherever you feel most creative.

We’re looking for confident creators who enjoy being on camera and know how to make content feel authentic. This is an ongoing role with a regular flow of video briefs for our clients’ social media advertising. You’ll work closely with our team, following clear briefs while bringing your own personality and ideas.

What you’ll do

  • Film engaging product demonstrations, talking-to-camera videos and lifestyle content.
  • Follow the creative brief and deliver clear, well-lit footage with good sound.
  • Communicate with our team and meet agreed deadlines.

What we’re looking for

You’re comfortable on camera, reliable and able to create high-quality content with your phone or camera. Previous UGC experience is welcome, and you can attach examples of your work below if you have them.

Pay & location

This is full-time, ongoing work, with a starting rate of $200–$300 per 60-second video. We’ll agree on the rate and expected video volume before you start. The role is fully remote, and creators from any country can apply.
